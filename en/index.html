About the Workshop

Leave behind everything that keeps you from writing

This workshop is designed for those who want to stop waiting for inspiration and build a steady writing habit. Over four weeks we write together, read, and give each other feedback. You finish with a completed story in hand.

✶

Small groups

Max 12 people. There's room for everyone's work, and everyone's voice is heard.

✎

One-on-one feedback

Every week you receive concrete, actionable feedback on the piece you write.

◐

Practice-focused

Not theory but practice. Every session includes writing exercises and live work.

♡

Supportive space

A judgment-free, encouraging environment. Beginners and experienced writers together.

Program

A four-week journey

Each week we go one step deeper into your story.

Week 1

Discover Your Story

We build observation skills, find your own voice, and establish a steady writing habit. Together we find the seed of your story.

  • Observation and note-taking
  • Finding your voice
  • Uncovering the idea/seed
Week 2

Dig Deeper Into the Story

We learn to build believable characters, lively dialogue, and conflict. We add layers to the story.

  • Character creation
  • Writing dialogue
  • Conflict and tension
Week 3

Develop Your Story

We work on plot, point of view, and scene-building. We strengthen the rhythm and flow of the story.

  • Plot
  • Point of view
  • Scene-building and rhythm
Week 4

Bring the Story Together

Editing, feedback, and finishing the piece. You complete your story and get it ready to share.

  • Editing techniques
  • Giving/receiving feedback
  • Finishing and sharing the piece
